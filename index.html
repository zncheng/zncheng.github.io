Biography

I am a Senior Software Engineer and Tech Lead at TikTok in London, working on multi-cloud content delivery infrastructure. My work focuses on CDN reliability, traffic management, security orchestration, and AI-assisted operations.

My research studies reliable distributed systems, data-center failures, and fault-tolerant stream processing. Recent collaborative work also explores indexing for disaggregated memory. I am interested in connecting systems research with the operational problems of large-scale cloud infrastructure.

Before joining TikTok in December 2024, I was a Senior Software Engineer at Tencent Cloud from April 2022 to December 2024. There, I designed service-mesh infrastructure for Tencent Serverless@Edge and developed self-healing capabilities for CDN caching systems.

During my Ph.D., I was a research intern at Alibaba Cloud from November 2020 to January 2022. I worked on predicting server failures from DRAM errors and on automated healing for cloud data centers. This work led to publications at SRDS 2022 and SRDS 2021, with publicly available code and a DRAM error dataset.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in March 2022, advised by Prof. Patrick P. C. Lee in the Applied Distributed Systems Lab. I received my master's degree from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2017, where I worked with Prof. Xi Li.
